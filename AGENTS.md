# Synthetic Society Lab operating instructions

Scope: this entire monorepo. These instructions implement the owner's autonomous research request and record existing authority; they do not create additional spending, access or publication authority.

The owner's November 5 instruction delegates routine decisions and justified, bounded recovery without repeated permission requests. Follow `.cursor/rules/responsible-autonomy.mdc` throughout the project. Keep the existing combined $5 spending ceiling; the owner did not specify an increase. Record reasons, enforce caps and preserve evidence before proceeding.

- Read `AUTONOMOUS_RESEARCH_ORCHESTRATION.md`, `CODEX_MASTER_EXECUTION.md`, the November 3 audit and the latest `reports/STATUS.md` before acting. Inspect actual files and rerun relevant checks; written claims alone are not evidence.
- The lead owns coordination and the completion dashboard. Use at most three specialized workers concurrently. Each task has explicit file ownership and a separate auditor. Workers must not edit one another's files or certify their own milestones.
- Prioritize E02 and the independent E01 retrospective statistical baseline. E03/E04 remain gated. Implement the full original completion contract; blocked gates remain blocked rather than being redefined as done.
- Every advanced condition requires a simpler matched comparator; swarms require equal-compute independent controls. Freeze research questions, scoring, sample and exclusions before confirmatory runs.
- Default unapproved paid work to zero. Existing authorization is a combined $5 cumulative ceiling, with the current E02 pilot limited to $1 and E01 paid inference at $0; count settled charges and outstanding holds together. Do not self-authorize increases, treat supplied credentials as approval, or weaken reservation/reconciliation controls. Use only OpenRouter for network model inference.
- Keep credentials and raw respondent/publisher data out of Git, reports and external services. Do not print keys. Rotation, third-party permissions and external publication require the owner's action or approval.
- Use documented permitted data sources. Do not bypass logins, ToS, paywalls or restricted feeds. Record source dates, rights, hashes and provenance. No named-voter profiling, targeting, outreach or claims of human persuasion from synthetic responses.
- E01 is retrospective unless independent pre-results evidence proves otherwise. Keep prediction inputs separate from outcomes; never backdate, choose on outcomes, or invent missing real values.
- MOCK/DEMO outputs must remain visibly labeled. Engineering smoke tests do not prove scientific effects, representative populations or external validity.
- Record run IDs, code/input hashes, seeds, actual serving provider, usage, costs, errors and exclusions. Preserve failed configurations. Refuse unknown billing, silent fallback and unbounded retries.
- Workers return the task contract listed in the orchestration document. Lead updates `reports/STATUS.md`; independent auditor records PASS/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN with direct evidence. Do not edit the auditor's findings to improve status.
- Complete all unblocked work, including reproducibility, visualization, paper/blog drafts and limitations. Never publish externally without approval. Do not ask about routine implementation choices already covered by these rules.
