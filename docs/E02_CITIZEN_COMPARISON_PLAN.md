## Finite rounding perturbations: nine citizen-target scenarios

Use one central scenario plus exactly eight deterministic directional scenarios. These deterministically perturb the rounded S0501 input weights within the declared±0.05percentage-point neighborhoods and then apply the declared normalization. They yield feasible derived normalized target margins under that method; they are not certified feasible latent ACS percentage vectors or substituted source observations. All source bytes remain unchanged. No count estimates or published25+ anchors are perturbed in this design.

The central scenario is the separately audited citizen target converter's unperturbed result. Each directional scenario applies the same named transfer to every included state and both C02/C04 citizenship columns, using the source percentages for that state/column. Exactly one transfer pair is active in each scenario; all other percentages stay at their original values. Exclude PR exactly as in the audited converter and preserve it in acquisition evidence.

The eight predefined scenarios are:

1. AGE_007_TO_008: transfer from25–44 row007 to45–54 row008.
2. AGE_008_TO_007: reverse the preceding transfer.
3. AGE_009_TO_010: transfer from55–64 row009 to65–74 row010.
4. AGE_010_TO_009: reverse the preceding transfer.
5. EDU_040_TO_041: transfer from high-school row040 to some-college/associate row041.
6. EDU_041_TO_040: reverse the preceding transfer.
7. EDU_041_TO_042: transfer from some-college/associate row041 to bachelor's row042.
8. EDU_042_TO_041: reverse the preceding transfer.

These pairs cross the existing coarsened age or education group boundaries. They intentionally avoid transfers within one coarsened bin that would leave every target unchanged. They are fixed before scores, not chosen because a direction helps either model.

For each state/column, use amplitude delta=min(0.04, p_donor,100−p_receiver) percentage points. Apply p'_donor=p_donor−delta and p'_receiver=p_receiver+delta with Decimal arithmetic. All other values remain unchanged. This is domain-safe clipping to the predeclared minimum, not post-score target adjustment. Since delta<=0.04<0.05, each perturbed cell remains strictly within or on the allowed domain-clipped rounding interval around its original displayed percentage. Require donor/receiver distinct and part of the reviewed same section and citizenship column. A zero feasible amplitude is retained and explicitly logged, never silently dropped or replaced by another pair.

Each transfer preserves that state/column's original rounded-input section sum exactly. The age-derived raw25+ discrepancy therefore remains the central scenario's discrepancy; education sums also retain their original rounding offset. Apply the same independently reviewed normalization to each perturbed composition, anchoring both age and education to that column's published row038 total. Add native+naturalized columns only after per-column normalization, then states to regions. Thus every scenario preserves each published25+ anchor, state citizen25+ total, regional total and national total. Age and education margin sets retain identical region shares and common national mass. The preserved education sums can remain99.8..100.1 rather than100, and the age inputs need not exactly reproduce the published25+ fraction N/T. Consequently, these input vectors are not certified jointly feasible underlying percentages, even when every component lies inside its numerical rounding neighborhood. Feasibility here refers only to nonnegative derived normalized targets that preserve the declared anchors and common mass. Normalization is a modeling/reconciliation operation and does not prove that those targets correspond to a latent ACS vector. This design does not normalize an all-lower or all-upper vector assembled from componentwise bounds.

Persist an audit trail for every scenario/state/column: original and perturbed selected percentages, pair, delta, any zero/clipped-amplitude reason, original/new section sums, unchanged count anchors, normalized targets and checksum. Validate every perturbed percentage against its original interval and domain, all mass equalities within the previously reviewed Decimal tolerance, and all source/target consistency checks. Compare the resulting normalized component shares against the central converter's componentwise bounds as an additional check, not as a recipe to construct a margin vector. Do not modify the acquisition verifier's one-decimal source validation to accept fabricated source precision: source validation happens first; a separate reviewed scenario layer allows precisely these labeled hypothetical perturbations.

A test-only tiny fixture should hand-check a non-clipped transfer, a donor-zero transfer, a receiver-at100 zero-amplitude transfer and an unclipped receiver99.9 transfer. With one-decimal source percentages and amplitude0.04, clipping yields either0 or0.04; do not invent higher-precision source values merely to exercise a partial-amplitude case. Invalid sign, wrong section/column, changed section sum, exceeded rounding interval, changed anchor or incoherent region mass must fail closed. The literal amplitude0.04 and the eight pair/direction IDs must be frozen before implementation and may change only through a new reviewed amendment.

## Limits and nonpromotion

The eight coordinated directions explore a small declared subset of rounded-input weight perturbations followed by the fixed normalization. They do not explore every state/column independently, all age/education pairs, simultaneous directions, count rounding, covariance uncertainty or the full feasible target space. Domain clipping may reduce perturbations in some cells. The observed range is not a worst-case bound, robustness certificate or ACS sampling confidence interval. Componentwise bounds from the target converter are likewise not jointly attainable full target vectors.

The bootstrap is descriptive resampling on an already-inspected ANES holdout, conditional on fixed ACS point targets/scenarios. It is not full survey-design uncertainty and includes no covariance-adjusted ACS benchmark uncertainty, temporal transport error, coverage/nonresponse error or model-response validation. Native+naturalized citizen25+ targets alone do not establish representative profiles, full nationwide/state coverage, causal human persuasion or prospective election performance. No automatic promotion follows a lower loss, a narrower finite scenario range, exact marginal convergence or full support imposed by smoothing. Preserve simpler baselines and negative findings. Further research needs independent data and review.
