# Analysis plans and scoring evidence

This index implements the named blueprint runbook; it is not external preregistration. Versioned protocols/configurations and independently accepted receipts define the current designs. A hash lock is definition integrity, not proof that a researcher never saw outcomes.

E01: signed Democratic-minus-Republican conditional two-party margin in percentage points. Compare the same race IDs, office, outcome denominator, cutoff and missing-prediction policy. Macro MAE, signed errors and paired absolute-error differences are descriptive. Never convert margins into invented probabilities/Brier scores. House boundaries require a verified historical crosswalk; regular Senate same-seat persistence normally uses six years. An independent winner can lie outside the D–R denominator. The accepted 30-state historical diagnostic's leave-one-state-out range is sensitivity, not a confidence interval. Original 2026 and agent comparisons remain separate requirements.

E02: the frozen initial primary outcome is fixed-prior in-party minus out-party warmth using two ANES party thermometers. The primary contrast is paired C2-minus-C1 before/after change. Independents remain in quality/absolute-gap reporting and are excluded from this primary by definition. Invalid/missing/refusal values are recorded, not imputed; cached changes must agree with independently recomputed raw answers. Instrument wording, ordering, assignments, exclusions and exact pre/post contexts are bound by the v3 definitions. Other political constructs cannot be claimed from this scale alone.

The fully crossed mocked engineering pilot uses paired seeds and isolated contexts. Its authored effect and degenerate interval are fixtures. Profile/stimulus crossed resampling is not evidence of human effects, and a three-text pool does not establish broad stimulus generalization. No real-model pilot variance or confirmatory sample-size calculation exists.

Population development: compare identical held-out records/weights and the same scoring floor; report support, exclusions, concentration and calibration as well as log loss. The inspected holdout remains exploratory. Original sparse suppression and unfavorable hierarchical results are preserved. Citizen25+ conversion is approximate, with distinct age/education denominators. Rounded-input perturbations create normalized derived targets; they are neither certified latent ACS vectors nor sampling intervals. The new comparison requires its own extractor/scenario/coverage audit before fitting. Original adult/state scope and the ANES/ACS group-quarters mismatch remain explicit.

Each advanced condition requires a simpler matched comparator; swarms additionally need equal-compute independent controls. Freeze all primary/secondary/sensitivity families before confirmatory execution. No outcome-driven race/profile selection, best-tau selection, missing-method replacement or silent denominator change. External registration, real-model power/robustness and full original research acceptance remain NOT_RUN/BLOCKED until separately evidenced.
