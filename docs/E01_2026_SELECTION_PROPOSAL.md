# Proposed retrospective E01 2026 selection/source amendment

**DRAFT FOR INDEPENDENT METHODS REVIEW — NOT ADOPTED, NOT EXECUTED.** Prepared November 3, 2026. This is a new proposal that fills gaps identified in `reports/E01_2026_READINESS.md`; it is not evidence of earlier registration. It changes no code, configuration, protocol, frozen manifest or result. No races are named or selected here. The original contract remains 24 House plus up to eight Senate broad races and four House plus two Senate deep races, with E01 explicitly retrospective unless an independently verified pre-results submission already exists.

## 1. Proposed frame and source lock

Use the complete permitted Cook November 2, 2026 House and Senate race-level ratings edition, with its original category and partisan designation preserved. The clipped local PDFs do not meet this requirement. A complete accessible/licensed export, coverage check and authenticated as-of evidence are prerequisites. No ratings are inferred from finance files, previous winners, neighboring rows, clipped text, numerical margins, other forecasters or eventual returns. No ratings source is silently substituted. If the required edition is unavailable, mark selection BLOCKED; a different source/date requires a separately versioned amendment and review before any selection or scoring.

The frame contains all regular November 3, 2026 voting U.S. House constituencies and regular Senate seats listed in that edition, not a handpicked competitive shortlist. Exclude nonvoting delegates, specials and runoffs under the current protocol. House IDs use `2026-SS-HOUSE-DD` with at-large `00`; Senate IDs include verified seat class. Official state records, not FEC registrations, establish election date, regular/general status, candidate names, parties and amendments. Collapse genuine fusion lines to one candidate under a reviewed mapping. Require exactly one named Democratic-affiliated and one Republican-affiliated candidate; log same-party top-two, no-major-party and uncontested structural exclusions explicitly. Other candidates do not make a contest ineligible, but D-R is a conditional target and is not necessarily the winning contest.

Missing or ambiguous ballot/rating evidence is **pending**, not an exclusion. Every frame contest must be resolved as eligible or a justified structural exclusion before selection. A missing state, source row or amendment blocks a claim of a complete universe. Publish frame coverage counts, exclusions and pending rows without outcomes. Do not use availability of polling, historical votes, spatial crosswalks, model answers or favorable errors to choose the sample; missing execution inputs for selected races subsequently block those runs, not trigger replacements.

For balancing, use the rating source's explicit D/R designation for lean/likely/solid. For a toss-up, use the source's explicit D-held/R-held seat grouping, with independently dated official seat-party corroboration if needed. These are sampling labels, not candidate-party or forecast assertions; record the distinction. A vacant seat uses its last holder's party as of the cutoff only when that rule and source evidence resolve it. Independent/other-held toss-ups remain in the frame with a separate `OTHER` label; their proposed quota is zero and this limitation must be reported. Unresolved labels are pending, never guessed. Independent methods review must accept this asymmetry or amend it before implementation.

## 2. Broad quotas, deterministic selection and shortfalls

House target: 24, allocating eight toss-ups, eight lean, four likely and four solid. Within each band split the target equally between D and R sampling labels: 4/4, 4/4, 2/2, 2/2. This deliberately emphasizes competitive contests and does not estimate nationwide average performance.

Senate target: up to eight, allocating two per band, one D and one R. Senate is its own frame and optimization; never pool offices to satisfy a quota. `OTHER` has zero quota in the nominal design for both offices but can enter only through the explicit shortfall rule below, not by relabeling. At-large House races are ordinary House units.

Freeze seed `20261103`, algorithm identifier `E01_2026_SELECTION_PROPOSAL_v1`, canonical IDs and exact UTF-8 serialization before running selection. For each contest compute SHA-256 of `E01_2026_SELECTION_PROPOSAL_v1|20261103|OFFICE|RACE_ID`; sort ascending by hexadecimal digest, breaking an exact digest tie by canonical race ID. Do not reroll the seed, change a snapshot, or repeat selection to obtain preferred names. Save all candidate ranks, not just selected ranks.

Select each office's subset using the following ordered objectives; later objectives cannot worsen earlier ones:

1. Set N to 24 House and min(8, eligible Senate count). If fewer than 24 eligible House contests exist, select all as an explicitly incomplete diagnostic and mark the original House sample requirement BLOCKED.
2. Require at least one selected contest from each of the four bands when that band has any eligible contest and N allows it. If a band is absent from the universe, record the shortage; do not invent ratings or claim four-band completion.
3. Minimize the sum, over the eight D/R band cells and four OTHER band cells, of absolute deviations between selected counts and nominal quotas (OTHER quotas are zero). This is the prespecified shortfall reallocation: available contests may fill shortages, retaining the closest feasible quota allocation. No cell is silently omitted from the receipt.
4. Minimize total absolute D-versus-R sampling-label imbalance within that office. OTHER remains separate, not split between D and R.
5. Maximize distinct Census regions, then distinct states represented. Use a frozen four-region state mapping. These are geographic-diversity preferences, not representative sampling weights; no map or vote data enters them.
6. Among equally optimal subsets, choose the one with the lexicographically smallest sorted list of contest ranks from the fixed hash ordering.

This specifies a unique subset and deterministic fallback without hand selection. Implementation must preserve the full objective vector, counts and proof of optimality or an independently verified exhaustive/reference result on fixtures. A heuristic returning an unproven feasible solution cannot claim it implements this specification. Independent review must verify feasibility and tie behavior before real selection. If a complete permitted universe cannot be assembled, none of these rules authorizes partial-universe sampling.

## 3. Six deep races, fixed before prediction runs

Choose four House contests from the selected broad sample: exactly one per band. Minimize `abs(n_D-2) + abs(n_R-2)` to prefer a 2-D/2-R sampling-label split (OTHER does not count toward either target), then maximize distinct regions, then distinct states, then use the same hash-rank tie rule. These are ordered optimization objectives, with band coverage hard. If any band lacks a broad selected contest, the proposed four-band deep design is BLOCKED; no handpicked replacement is permitted.

Choose two Senate contests from the broad sample: one competitive (toss-up or lean) and one less competitive (likely or solid). Minimize `abs(n_D-1) + abs(n_R-1)` to prefer opposite D/R labels (OTHER counts toward neither target), then prefer different regions, then the fixed hash ordering. If either group is absent, choose the two available broad Senate contests with minimum `abs(n_D-1) + abs(n_R-1)`, then maximum regions, then rank, recording the exact fallback and absent group. With fewer than two broad Senate contests, six-race completion is BLOCKED; any smaller diagnostic remains explicitly incomplete.

Keep these IDs fixed even if a later arm fails, an exact crosswalk is missing, or results look unfavorable. No substitutes after input inspection or scoring. The six deep cases support bounded comparisons, not universal superiority or nationwide extrapolation.

## 4. Cutoffs, prediction/outcome separation and failure policy

Propose the new retrospective feature cutoff `2026-11-03T04:59:59Z` (November 2, 23:59:59 America/New_York). This is an analytical cutoff, not a claim that snapshots or forecasts existed then. Each input needs publication, first-observed, retrieval, revision, hash, source and reuse fields. The source display date alone cannot establish archived bytes. Missing valid as-of provenance blocks the corresponding arm; post-cutoff news, returns, exit polls, updated candidate financial records and retrospective ratings changes cannot enter the predictor. Flag the four known future-dated candidate-summary coverage records for adjudication; do not silently fix dates.

Freeze source receipts, frame/exclusions, policy/seed, sample/deep IDs, scoring, arms, feature transformations, exclusions, cost/call limits, model/provider identifiers and failure rules before predictions. Seal all prediction artifacts before the separate evaluator loads any 2026 target returns. The outcome custodian supplies certified final counts, certification date, stage, mappings and source hashes; partial/unofficial returns cannot silently become final targets. No made-up certification deadline is used. Subsequent source corrections require immutable old/new records and a dated deviation; do not mutate the frozen primary run. Post-cutoff candidate corrections affect validation and interpretation transparently rather than masquerading as pre-cutoff knowledge.

Researchers may already know outcomes; model training may contain them. A retrospective lock, separate process or file hash does not erase that contamination. Preserve this limitation even when execution isolation passes. No analyst chooses the sample, transformations or comparator from observed 2026 errors.

## 5. Same-race controls, estimands and earned complexity

S0 is prior same-seat D-R margin with frozen zero national/incumbency adjustments; House needs validated 2024-to-2026 geography, Senate the same seat six years earlier. Nonzero adjustments require an outcome-independent reviewed specification. S1 polling and S2 demographics run before agent arms; source unavailability is explicit NA/BLOCKED, not invented data. S1 is a parallel benchmark and need not be nested.

Freeze each planned arm's input availability before outcomes. Within every claim, compare predictions on exactly the same race IDs, outcome version, cutoffs and evaluation weights. Report broad S0 House and Senate results separately; recompute S0/S1/S2 on the identical six deep IDs for deep comparisons. Never compare 24/32-case baseline MAE with six-case agent MAE. If S1 lacks polls, a planned S1 comparison on the full target set is incomplete; a predeclared matched availability-subset analysis is secondary, with S0 recomputed there and all excluded IDs/reasons shown. No favorable post-result intersection selection.

Primary descriptive score is equal-weight race macro MAE in margin percentage points, separately by office; report toss-ups and all four bands separately with realized counts. Do not invent probability calibration/Brier scores from margins. Equal weights describe the selected cohort, not the national race universe; no nationwide estimator is claimed. Preserve failures and report success/coverage alongside scores. Missing predictions block the intended full-set comparison rather than receive a convenient score or replacement race. Any successful-pair subset analysis must be predeclared secondary, list all failures and avoid a complete-sample claim. No small correlated-cohort IID confidence claim; report paired errors and predeclared leave-one-race-out sensitivity as descriptive.

A0 direct forecasts, A1 independent profiles, A2 approved information, A3 assistant interaction and A4 interaction must each have a simpler matched comparator with the same allowable evidence. Demographics, real text, feeds and assistants require their separate validity/access gates. A4 additionally needs equal-compute independent controls with matched model/provider, input evidence, total input/output token envelope, calls and seeds; log actual usage, unused quota and differences rather than assert compute equality from metadata. An arm failure never licenses a weaker comparator. Default paid budget remains $0; a selection proposal grants no inference authority.

## 6. Acceptance and future prospective test

Before adoption, a methods reviewer separate from the author must accept or explicitly revise the frame/OTHER handling, quotas/shortfalls, deterministic solver objectives, geography preference, deep rules, cutoff, analysis/failure policy and source rights. Fixture tests must verify row-order invariance, no duplicate IDs, shortages, absent bands, OTHER labels, ties, region mapping, amendment precedence, missing evidence refusal and same-race enforcement. Independent source review must then confirm complete permitted ratings/ballots and as-of provenance. Save the reviewed proposal hash; only then implement a versioned protocol/config and select. This draft does not pass those gates itself.

A future prospective test would register an independently reviewed policy, complete input/sample lock and actually generated predictions in an independently timestamped archive before the relevant elections/results, using validated contemporary evidence and unexposed outcomes. It would obtain publication approval and any budget separately, preserve all omissions, and distinguish model-training uncertainty from evaluator blinding. Creating a later archive for this retrospective reconstruction cannot confer prospective status on 2026.

Current dependencies: complete permitted Cook edition and as-of evidence, official final lists plus amendments for the full frame, compatible historical inputs/crosswalks for selected contests, independent methods/source audit, and a valid later certified outcome source. No actual selection, inference, external publication or claim of scientific completion is authorized or performed.
