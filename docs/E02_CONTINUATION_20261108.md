# E02 continuation of the original limited pilot

The owner requested experiments and delegated routine bounded recovery. This
continuation completes the original sample: four fictional profiles, two
authored DEMO notices, and the matched C1 news/C2 social wrappers. The instrument,
assignments, contents, seeds, scoring and exclusions remain frozen in
`configs/e02_exploratory_live_pilot_v1_1.json`.

The November 5 run dispatched sixteen phases and stopped at a billing HTTP404.
Fifteen phases committed normally. Its sixteenth response already exists; a
separate metadata recovery verifies that same response, never resending its
prompt. The original stopped report, journal, financial rows and holds remain
unchanged. The successor records the recovered answer separately, then starts
at unit 8 pre and ends at unit 15 post: at most sixteen new requests.

The combined owner limit is $5; this pilot's cumulative sublimit is $1. Original
settled charges and all three outstanding holds count toward both. The shared
owner starts with eighteen lifetime dispatches and permits at most thirty-four.
No purchases, top-ups, new owners, budget resets or model POST retries occur.
Historical 340 + 340 + 788 microdollar holds remain conservative reservations
even after the new evidence establishes the last response's exact bill.

The same OpenRouter model and Azure route are used. Fresh reviewed pricing has
unchanged monetary terms; the runtime rechecks a public catalog. Every phase
retains its 120-second deadline, at most 512 output tokens, and a conservative
input reservation. Billing permits at most three GETs per new generation,
waiting 10, 10 and 20 seconds before successive attempts, and retries only an
observed HTTP404 with successful connection closure. A new uncertain dispatch
stops the continuation; all responses and failed dispositions remain retained.

Acceptance criteria:

- Billing recovery: same generation/provider/model, exact response-versus-bill
  cost equality, native usage within the original envelope, bounded GETs, zero
  POSTs, unchanged owner and original journal, independent evidence review.
- Engineering: mocked recovery/continuation and real isolated-owner integration
  pass; changed ancestry, duplicate requests, excess usage/cost, expired leases,
  unsupported activity and exhausted limits stop. Independent source review
  precedes actual execution.
- Execution: exact reviewed caller and continuation bundle, append-only ancestry
  on the same owner, at most sixteen new phases, complete durable dispositions
  and provider billing for every accepted response.
- Results: recompute the original analysis from the authenticated combined
  responses, report all missing/invalid units, costs, uncertainty and provenance;
  independently audit the retained outcome before declaring the pilot complete.

This is an exploratory model experiment with authored stimuli. The original
and continuation batches are separated in time; identical request settings do
not prove identical model internals or temporal conditions. Report that gap.
One model family, eight matched blocks and no human-study anchor cannot establish
human political persuasion, population representativeness or forecasting skill.
