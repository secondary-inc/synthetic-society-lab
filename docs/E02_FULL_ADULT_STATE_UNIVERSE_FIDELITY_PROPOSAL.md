# Original nationwide adult/state universe fidelity: methods proposal

November 3, 2026. Status: METHODS ONLY, PENDING INDEPENDENT REVIEW. This new document proposes how to preserve the original broader nationwide adult/state objective. It does not change any frozen protocol, N0/N1 config, source parser, exclusion rule, experiment/analysis code or existing finding. No person GET, enrollment, person parse, demographic fit, model call or new human study was performed. Root's separate live-survey bridge is outside this document's implementation ownership and does not itself identify a human-population estimand.

## Recommendation: full adult accounting plus a nested overlap cohort

Retain **all adult residents represented by the 2020–2024 ACS PUMS in the 50 states and DC**, including noncitizens and both institutional and noninstitutional group quarters, as the candidate full demographic universe F. Here “full” describes intended source coverage, not a complete census, a November2026 population or guaranteed coverage of every resident. Keep Puerto Rico separately recorded and outside the project's currently specified50-state/DC geography; do not erase it from source provenance or call that choice all U.S. jurisdictions.

Define a separately labeled nested cohort C: citizens aged18+ living in households in those same51 jurisdictions. C approximates one dimension of ANES fresh-sample frame overlap; it is not all adults, all adult citizens, all eligible voters, registered voters, likely voters or an independently validated state political population. Publish neither C-only outputs nor C-renormalized weights as estimates for F. Demographic inclusion in F and political-response identification are different requirements: representing a person's demographic stratum does not supply their attitudes or information-treatment response.

The proposed method keeps F intact when political data are missing. It does not fill missing political priors with ANES citizens, a neutral score, invented party categories, model stereotypes or synthetic certainty. Unknown outcomes can make the original scientific objective remain BLOCKED even after demographic accounting works; that is an honest unresolved dependency, not permission to replace F with C.

## What the cached primary sources establish

The retained Census2020–2024 dictionary defines RT=P, STATE, AGEP0–99 (topcoded), CIT1–4 citizen and5 noncitizen, RELSHIPP20–36 household relationships,37 institutional group quarters and38 noninstitutional group quarters. SCHL01–24 supports the existing coarse education categories for adults, including18–24; blank is a documented under3 category, not a valid adult education value. PWGTP is a person weight; PWGTP1..80 are signed replicate weights. The retained five-year user guide PDFpage4 explicitly describes person records that include group quarters. Thus the person schema can support the broader demographic partition without a housing merge, identifying households or retaining SERIALNO/PUMA. This is schema feasibility, not an executed full-adult benchmark.

The cached ANES May19,2026 user-guide/codebook PDFpages6–8 defines fresh-sample eligibility as U.S. citizenship, age18+ at recruitment and residence at a sampled residential address. Fresh in-person coverage is48 contiguous states/DC; fresh web coverage is50states/DC. Both exclude institutional/group quarters. Its panel has a different earlier target population; it cannot be added indiscriminately to patch missing coverage. The guide mentions a GSS re-interview component targeting adult residents but states that those data are not included in this release. No unacquired GSS supplement or its item comparability is presumed available here.

Cached ANES feasibility evidence identifies public sample-location state codes, younger adults and sparse state/age support. Sample location is not proof of current residence, voter registration or district eligibility. The independently recorded ANES FAQ clarification cautions that national probability sampling does not establish representative state estimates. No PUMS state margin repairs that political-response limitation by itself.

## Future benchmark eligibility and accounting specification

A separately reviewed future aggregation amendment would validate the required person-record domains and weights for every input record before applying the adult predicate AGEP>=18. Record every under18 exclusion and every invalid/missing-required-field failure. Do not silently delete malformed adults or assign invalid citizenship/relationship codes to households. The original published16,095,728 national person records are an all-age source-integrity check, not an adult-population estimate or the count of synthetic profiles.

For valid adults, retain two citizenship categories (CIT1–4 / CIT5) crossed with three residence categories (household20–36 / institutional37 / noninstitutional38). This yields six mutually exclusive domains, of which citizen-household is C. Accumulate the same four age groups and three education groups within each of51 states: up to3,672 demographic cells, each with point totals, unweighted support, squared-weight sums, allocation diagnostics and80 matching signed replicate totals. Zero-valued cells stay present with their precision/support flags. No rule may drop a state or entire domain for sparsity.

Derive the612-cell full-adult age/education table by summing the six domains. Derive the separately labeled612-cell C table from its single domain. C is already included in F: never concatenate their records or add their totals. Enforce each state's F=sum(six domains), national F=sum(all51states), C<=F for point counts/positive point weights, and matching identities separately for every replicate sum. Signed replicate components need not satisfy point-weight ordering or positivity; do not clip them to force it.

Use the released five-year person weights without dividing by five again. Apply corresponding replicate indices across disjoint same-release states and recompute each numerator and denominator before SDR variance/covariance. Never infer independence by adding separately estimated state variances. Preserve zero-SE/estimate warnings and block unsupported ratio intervals with nonpositive replicate denominators. These quantify conditional sampling variability, not total error, coverage correction, ANES transport or human validity.

The current N1/Wyoming parser deliberately excludes noncitizens and group quarters for C. Its existing excluded totals use an exclusive exclusion order and do not retain the full age/education/citizenship/residence cross-classification. They cannot reconstruct F or its six domains. Preserve all old outputs and negative comparisons as C/other-original-universe evidence. A new versioned parser/benchmark schema and resource/control review are necessary before broader aggregation. The sixfold demographic-cell expansion is not implicitly approved by accepted612-cell limits. This document changes neither current code nor current source access authority.

## Missingness, support and transport must remain explicit

Maintain distinct statuses for missing source/jurisdiction, invalid source value, structural zero target mass, positive target mass with no relevant survey support, very low survey support, item missing/refusal, and an item undefined for the chosen population. None is equivalent to a measured zero effect. A missing state archive blocks complete national accounting; it does not create a zero-weight state. Unweighted source/survey counts, weighted mass, effective support and missingness rates answer different questions and must not be substituted for one another.

For C, ANES may supply baseline opinion information only subject to its sample selection, design weights, item availability, complete-data rules, geography, mode and period limitations. Even inside C, absent state-age cells remain unsupported. The existing feasibility audit found younger respondents missing in12state/DC units and held-out records absent in3states. Those are preserved support findings, not a license to choose a better split or invent local priors. Existing inspected holdouts remain exploratory. A new state political prior inferred by pooling is an assumption to test, not observed state representation.

For the five domains outside C, ANES fresh-sample political priors are outside its coverage and remain UNIDENTIFIED. Causal human information effects are not supplied even inside C: ANES baseline observations are not randomized human responses to these stimuli. PUMS supplies demographic distributions, not party identification, feeling thermometers, media exposure, factual beliefs or treatment effects. Citizenship and residence category must not be used to manufacture those missing variables.

Period and measurement transport also remain: ACS2020–2024 period estimates, ANES recruitment/election-day age and a proposed2026 experiment do not share one reference instant. PUMS age perturbation/topcoding, confidentiality edits, imputation and sampling can affect cells. National survey nonresponse, household/address coverage and mode differences are not cured by calibration. Actual schooling can be benchmarked across18+ with PUMS; S0501's25+-education margins cannot be relabeled full-adult education targets. If race, income or more detailed associations are desired later, their person/household units and universes need separate source review; do not expand fields opportunistically.

## Estimands and what can be reported

Keep three separately named objects:

1. **Demographic accounting for F and each domain.** Weighted distributions/counts and their conditional source uncertainty for the specified source period, with no political-response interpretation.
2. **ANES baseline/overlap diagnostics for C.** Source/survey support and explicitly assumption-dependent transport comparisons. A C-conditioned estimate is labeled C and is never silently population-renormalized to F. This still is not state political validation.
3. **Synthetic experimental responses under a frozen generator/prompt.** Randomized differences in AI outputs for precisely recorded fictional profiles, treatment and model/provider conditions. If an unsupported prior is left unknown, the prompt records that unknown; the resulting output is a model behavior under missing information, not evidence filling the human-data gap. Equal engineering allocation, population weighting and domain-specific weighting are distinct estimands.

The existing primary in-party minus out-party thermometer contrast requires a fixed party prior and valid pre/post answers. For unknown/nonpartisan party it is undefined or excluded according to its frozen rule. It cannot be made an all-adult primary merely by assigning party from demographics. Primary exclusions need their own numerator/denominator and target-mass accounting; their total is not silently treated as the whole adult population. No original primary outcome or preregistered/confirmatory claim is amended here.

A future full-adult outcome must be defined for every intended domain before an experiment. Candidate exploratory outcomes such as the raw Democratic/Republican thermometers or their absolute gap do not require an assigned in-party label, but survey validity and human evidence remain separate. Choosing such an outcome would require an independently reviewed protocol amendment, not a post-result workaround. Missing/invalid AI answers remain missing rather than neutral; report assignment, valid-pair and domain/target-weight coverage separately.

For a genuinely well-defined outcome, the identity theta_F=pi_C*theta_C+sum_g(pi_g*theta_g) over the five other domains makes unidentified terms visible. Target masses pi would come from a later approved demographic benchmark; they are not calculated here. If domain effects are unidentified, do not drop their masses, normalize C to1, or report a full-adult point effect. Bounds are possible only with a valid common outcome range and an explicitly justified known component; they are not estimates of unmeasured effects. For example, an absolute0–100 thermometer-gap difference-in-changes has logical range[-200,200], but even theta_C lacks human causal evidence here. Current data therefore do not justify narrower full-adult human-effect bounds from synthetic effects. This illustration defines no new primary outcome and fabricates no numeric result.

## Comparators, fictional sampling and earned complexity

A full demographic accounting model can generate fictional state/age/education/citizenship/residence labels while leaving unsupported political fields unknown. It must not clone source individuals, infer named people's traits, assume a realistic feed or claim demographic labels establish realistic behavior. Draw only from reviewed aggregates with recorded sampling probabilities/design weights; preserve all positive-mass state/domain strata. A12-profile mock cannot cover51states, much less all state/domain cells. Coverage lower bounds are not power calculations or representative sample sizes.

Within C, compare a simpler regional/national transported-prior model with any proposed partially pooled state model under identical targets, eligible records, inspected holdout treatment, seeds, compute, scoring and exclusions. Preserve earlier hierarchy failures. Outside C, both models retain the same unknown statuses; one model cannot win by fabricating answers where the other honestly abstains. Report population-mass coverage/abstention alongside scores and state which comparisons are not identified. If auxiliary political evidence is later permitted, freeze its independent validation and overlap before use; none is acquired or presumed adequate here.

Real news, social feeds, assistant interaction and swarms remain earned-complexity extensions with simpler matched controls and, for swarms, equal-compute independent controls. They do not repair a missing human sampling frame or validate excluded populations by increasing agent count.

## Scientific acceptance gates and explicit blockers

U0: Independent review freezes F, the six disjoint domains, nested C, geography/reference period and the distinctions among all-adult, citizen, household and eligible-voter frames. Required before any target change. Original broader scope remains unwaived.

U1: New versioned full-adult benchmark/control amendment with authored partition/missingness/denominator/replicate/resource/source-integrity tests and independent review. All51 sources must eventually validate; current N1 acceptance remains separately pending. No new access or parsing follows this document.

U2: Independent demographic arithmetic/support/disclosure review before any full-adult target conversion or profile sampling. Retain unsupported states/domains and explicit unidentification. No household-C relabeling as F.

U3: Separate ANES domain/weights/design/missingness/transport specification and matched simpler-comparator validation for C. State political validity requires evidence beyond demographic margins and national survey state labels. Original sparse/negative findings and inspected-split limitations remain.

U4: Human political baselines outside C and human treatment-response validity remain BLOCKED without appropriate independent evidence covering those domains, comparable items and consent/rights. Any permitted future source or study requires its own review. No legal/eligibility proxy, unsolicited outreach, private linkage or autonomous recruitment is authorized. Until then keep these priors/effects unknown; do not claim completed full-adult political modeling.

U5: Freeze a domain-valid outcome, sampling/weights, sample/power/exclusions, sensitivity and uncertainty plan before any confirmatory experiment. Model inference, spending, external validation, scientific promotion and publication remain separately gated. A live-survey bridge or valid model response proves an engineering connection, not U3/U4/U5 acceptance.
