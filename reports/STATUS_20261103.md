## E02: what actually ran

The primary instrument is the two ANES party feeling thermometers (V241166/V241167) and fixed prior party identification V241221. Primary score is in-party minus out-party warmth for Democrat/Republican profiles. The contrast is C2 minus C1 in before/after change for the same profile × stimulus × repetition. Independent/unknown party profiles remain in quality/secondary reporting and are excluded from the primary by definition. The scale uses thermometer points; division by 100 is range normalization, not a standardized-deviation effect.

The real-source exploratory fit uses combined fresh pre-election weights and survey-cluster holdout. It matches 25+ ACS resident age and education margins within Census region over supported ANES joint cells. It converged in 246 iterations, with maximum marginal residual about 9.70e-9; 139 supported cells from 1,253 training records; effective survey sample size about 352; 446 held-out records; maximum calibration factor about 5.40; held-out party-share discrepancy about 5.70 percentage points. These are descriptive diagnostics, not human-survey treatment estimates.

Five-category education had structural zeros after the minimum-five-record cell rule; three categories were selected and documented before model execution. A further 1,033 training records were in suppressed sparse cells. Other exclusions were 2,416 records outside the selected weighted fresh sample and 373 outside/invalid age, education or region. Counts sum to all 5,521 input records when retained training and held-out records are included. Large support exclusions and citizen/resident universe mismatch prevent a representative-electorate claim. B03002 race and B19001 household-income files are retained as audit-only benchmarks; their universes are not silently imposed on 25+ person weights. No state-level effects are estimated.

Each pilot uses **12 fictional profiles × three DEMO stories × five arms = 180 units and 360 mocked pre/post calls**. There are 36 observations per arm, 24 matched primary pairs and 12 nonpartisan primary exclusions. The mock deliberately injects a known two-point social-wrapper gap change, which the scorer recovers. The [2,2] resampling interval is a deterministic fixture property and has no scientific significance. C3/C4 use matched scripted Q&A, not a live assistant.

## E01: what works and why real execution is blocked

S0 predicts prior same-seat Democratic-minus-Republican two-party margin plus explicit frozen national/incumbency adjustments, clamped to ±100. Its demonstration uses zero adjustments on two explicitly invented fixtures. The predictor child receives only validated features, runs in an empty temporary directory with a stripped environment, and installs a tested post-import Python audit hook denying file/network/process I/O. This protects trusted code from accidental reads, not adversarial code via an OS sandbox. Outcomes enter only the separate evaluator after prediction hashing. Local hashes do not independently timestamp a forecast or prove the operator never saw outcomes.

Two DEMO executions reproduce the frozen forecast hash, and a predeclared +1pp shift produces a saved same-race counterfactual. These are engineering fixtures, not actual election results or evidence of forecasting improvement.

Real audit scanned **2,193,786 rows** across three local MEDSL inputs. It found 19,323 redacted/missing and three invalid numeric Senate vote rows in 2024, plus 9,863 redacted/missing and four invalid numeric House rows. Mixed TOTAL/component modes and possible one-vote sentinels require source-specific adjudication; mixed state-level modes alone do not prove double counting.

Real execution is blocked because 2024 House lacks matching verified 2022 House inputs; 2022 and 2024 regular Senate contests are different seat classes (2018 is needed for a 2024 same-seat lag); official ballot manifests, exact geography versions/crosswalks and aggregation policies are incomplete. REAL prediction freezing fails closed. No actual forecast or MAE is fabricated.

## Shared controls, review and reproducibility

Runs preserve exact configs/seeds, code Git SHA plus uncommitted source-tree fingerprint, Python/platform, input hashes, response/request hashes, errors and checksum ledgers. UUIDs/timestamps distinguish instances; deterministic content hashes support replay. Jobs are idempotent, follow recorded randomized priority, and retain completion results. Interrupted RUNNING jobs require reconciliation and are not automatically replayed. Resume rejects changed code/config.

The OpenRouter adapter has fixed model/provider routing, fresh supplied provider pricing, max-price fields, bounded input/output, zero automatic retries/fallbacks and transactional total/module cost reservations. Unknown billing reserves the worst case and halts. Known charges survive malformed outputs; overrun charges are retained and halt future requests. These controls are mocked in tests, not verified against actual provider billing. Pricing acquisition, live credential validation and approved real-run orchestration remain NOT RUN. Both supplied credentials remain local, ignored and untracked.

Independent review checked ANES/ACS mappings and found two concrete bugs: dispatch order differed from assignment order, and a later parse failure could lose a reported charge. Both were corrected and regression-tested. Earlier development runs remain immutable and are superseded by the final evidence IDs above.

The local viewer is `data/processed/dashboard/index.html`; it can be rebuilt and opened without a server. Preview serving is loopback-only and permits only index/snapshot, not raw data or credentials. Underlying data, generated runs, wheel/clean environment and previews are not committed; a fresh clone can run fixture tests and the E01 DEMO but needs exact registered raw files for full fitting/audit. The tracked evidence JSON contains aggregate receipts, not underlying respondent or publisher data.
