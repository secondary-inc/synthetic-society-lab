## Design and original question

topics (library hours and transit access) yielded eight profile-topic units.
Eight fresh neutral/reference baselines preceded 32 posts in a 2×2 crossing of
NEWS/SOCIAL text-format labels with OMITTED/SHOWN own support. Each topic's
body text and source attribution were identical across all four cells. SHOWN
included only the exact own same-topic baseline support; OMITTED did not.
These reference baselines were exposed, not unexposed. No old answer was reused.

Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 served by Nebius through route `nebius/fp8` was the
single fixed configuration. Selection used earlier observed item completeness;
this is an exploratory, selected configuration rather than model-family
replication. The outcome is a custom 0–100 support item, not a validated human
scale. Text labels are not authentic news/social feeds, source credibility,
visual scrolling or personalized recommendation. Both topics supplied positive
fictional 40% visit/trip evidence; negative-valence generalization was not tested.
