# Alternative primary ratings sources: feasibility

November 3, 2026. Read-only documentary exploration; independent review pending. No 2026 returns, sample selection, model calls, payment, contact submission or external publication. Only this report and its JSON receipt are new. Cook collection was not revisited; the approved Cook proposal, selector and historical paths remain unchanged.

## Conclusion

Cook is **not the sole technical source route**: Inside Elections visibly offers House/Senate exports, and the Crystal Ball's own publication provides dated final-rating material. However, this check does **not** establish a complete permitted independently authenticated November 2 frame from any alternative. Exact remaining properties are recorded below. It would be premature either to adopt an alternative now or to describe a Cook license as the only possible external route. Source permission, as-of evidence, category compatibility and full-frame validation are separate dependencies.

## Inside Elections: strongest directly accessible export candidate

Its ordinary [House](https://insideelections.com/ratings/house/) and [Senate](https://insideelections.com/ratings/senate/) pages publicly expose Save/Print PDF, API and XML export links. The API links were followed exactly as offered; no endpoint was guessed and no login or restricted service was accessed. The House export declares total 435; Senate declares total 100, including out-of-cycle chamber context. Both declare last update October 29, 2026 at 13:05 -05:00; their generated times differ. The JSON carries election year, office/class, district, rating text, party/held-seat information and special flags. Ratings include Tilt, which the existing Cook-based policy lacks. [Offered House export](https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_house_year%3D2026_district%3Dall_clean.json?v=1793388069), [offered Senate export](https://insideelections.com/wp-content/themes/inside-elections/cache/ratings_latest_senate_year%3D2026_district%3Dall_clean.json?v=1793297246).

These are publisher-declared counts/times, not independently validated unique coverage, frozen bytes, or proof that there were no amendments through November 2. No missing race is assigned a default rating. Senate must be reconciled to regular 2026 ballot seats, not treated as 100 eligible elections. Do not use an incumbent-party field as the favored party of a directional rating. An explicit reviewed Tilt/category and toss-up held-party mapping is required. No full roster or raw export was saved, hashed or used for selection in this task.

The [publisher's About page](https://insideelections.com/about/) describes paid access to archives and full race/candidate pages and displays copyright. Searches within its public About/Data pages did not locate an explicit research reuse license. Public export visibility establishes an offered access route, not unlimited extraction, retention, republication or redistribution rights. No subscription, archive request or permission inquiry was submitted. Permission scope and an authenticated dated export remain to be established; this report does not conclude that permission is impossible or necessarily requires payment.

## UVA / Sabato's Crystal Ball: dated primary publication, incomplete verification

The primary [2026 House](https://centerforpolitics.org/crystalball/2026-house/) and [2026 Senate](https://centerforpolitics.org/crystalball/2026-senate/) pages returned reader HTTP 403. An ordinary House browser visit encountered a security verification page. No CAPTCHA/security challenge was solved or bypassed and that tab was closed.

The publisher's own accessible [final-ratings article](https://sabatoscrystalball.substack.com/p/the-final-2026-ratings-democrats), by Kyle Kondik and J. Miles Coleman, displays November 2, 2026, House/Senate aggregate forecast totals, a House-ratings table heading and Senate-ratings map heading. This is primary authored forecasting material, not an observed election result or a third-party reconstruction. The text-only view did not provide a complete uniquely keyed race-level frame; image/map completeness, regular-versus-special seats, full category/party mapping, reuse terms and independent as-of bytes were not verified. No omitted district was inferred safe/solid. Search snippets and aggregator mirrors were discovery only, not accepted ratings data.

A change to this source requires a reviewed category/date amendment. Final directional calls may leave fewer or no toss-ups; inspect and disclose the actual published distribution rather than switch to an earlier edition to fill quotas or claim the four-band deep design is complete. Neither university affiliation nor a free article establishes a reusable-data license. No entire article or ratings image was cached or redistributed.

## Split Ticket: primary route checked, 2026 full frame not located here

The public [data repository](https://split-ticket.org/data-repository/) lists historical WAR/SHAVE resources and 2024 forecasts; the inspected page did not identify a 2026 House/Senate ratings export. Its own [partnership announcement](https://split-ticket.org/2025/08/19/whats-next-split-ticket-partners-with-the-argument/) directs much future analysis to The Argument. Those primary pages do not establish a November 2, 2026 full ratings frame, category/party universe, authenticated snapshot or applicable reuse license. Historical datasets and a source's partnership do not authorize inferring missing 2026 ratings. No historical-return dataset, paywall or subscriber channel was opened for this check. This is a bounded negative finding, not proof no suitable publication exists elsewhere.

## Required amendment and acceptance before any use

A methods/source reviewer must separately accept an alternative publisher, exact edition and as-of rule, permission scope, complete expected voting-contest universe, ballot/amendment joins, canonical IDs and category/party mapping. Preserve the original Cook policy and record why a replacement is proposed. A snapshot with only competitive races needs an explicit publisher rule for omitted seats plus independently verified coverage; no such default was verified here. A current export's self-reported timestamp is recorded as publisher evidence, not upgraded to independent historical authentication.

Then test duplicate IDs, missing states/races, non-cycle/special Senate context, unrelated office rows, same-party ballots, unknown labels, absent bands and any new Tilt/Safe mapping before freezing a reviewed frame. Do not cherry-pick a source/date because its races or eventual errors look better.
